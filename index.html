Hallo~ Thank you for visiting this page!

My name is Cobby. You might encounter me with many other name like Cobbi, Cobbie, cobbyproto, Bao Luu (facebook) but feel free to call me however ya want <3

I'm currently 20. A Vietnamese and currently living in Ho Chi Minh City.

I'm quite introvert so I usually dont talk much online and I might look very shy and quiet if you meet me at first. But feel free to interact with me, I might be talking alot after a while hehe.

Joining this fandom in 2021, but still I'm still very new to furry community hehe. After starting to attend SEA furcons. I have develop my love for this fandom more than ever. I really enjoy arts in many forms, from dancing to singing and drawings. It help me feel more connected to my character and somewhat can express myself more.

Thanks for reading my little yapping about me hehe x3 If you still interested you can scroll down for more~
